Design and prototype a complete, modern, mobile-first digital platform for PS-26089: Cooperative Gig Services Platform for Household & Community Services.

The product is a cooperative-owned workforce marketplace and workforce management platform connecting households, businesses, contractors, cooperative societies, and local workers such as maids, cooks, cleaners, security guards, porters, electricians, plumbers, carpenters, painters, drivers, caregivers, gardeners, mehendi artists, hair stylists, beauticians, construction workers, daily-wage labourers, and other local service providers.

The product should NOT look like a clone of Urban Company, Uber, Swiggy, or other gig platforms.

The core positioning is:

“Book. Work. Earn. Grow — powered by cooperatives.”

The platform should combine:

Service marketplace
Worker discovery
Worker onboarding
Cooperative membership
Worker verification
AI-powered matching
Travel-aware scheduling
Workforce availability
Recurring employment
Daily-wage labour marketplace
Worker mobility
Demand forecasting
Workforce shortage detection
Cooperative-to-cooperative workforce sharing
Payments
Digital invoices
Worker welfare
Reviews
Communication
Notifications
Worker safety
Governance
Analytics

Create a polished SIH-level prototype with realistic data and a complete clickable user journey.

The strongest visual story should be:

A local worker gets verified → receives work → completes it → earns transparently → builds reputation → gets welfare/training → cooperative sees demand → AI detects a shortage → workers are redistributed from a low-demand area → more citizens get work and more customers get services.
